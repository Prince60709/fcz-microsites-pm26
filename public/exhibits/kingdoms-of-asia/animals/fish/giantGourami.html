Giant Gourami

Common Names: Common Giant Gourami, Kaloi, Warship Fish Status: Least Concern / Stable (widely farmed)

Range: Native to Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Mekong Basin); introduced globally. Habitat: Warm, slow-moving rivers, swamps, lakes, canals, and flooded marshes with dense vegetation.

Size & Weight: 18-28 inches long (up to 30+ inches); up to 22 lbs (10 kg). Lifespan: 10-20+ years in human care.

Predators & Conservation:

Predators: Juveniles are eaten by large birds, crocodilians, otters, and predatory fish. Humans are the primary threat to adults via commercial harvesting. Conservation: While local wild populations face heavy fishing pressure, the species thrives globally through aquaculture. In many Asian cultures, specimens with large head humps are prized as symbols of good luck.

Physical Description

    Males: Larger and more streamlined with pointed dorsal and anal fins. They develop a prominent forehead hump with age and turn nearly black during spawning.

    Females: Smaller with a rounder body and less extended, rounded fins.
